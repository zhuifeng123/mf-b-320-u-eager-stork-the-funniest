The Funniest Toaster Error Message

Write the funniest error message a kitchen toaster would show its owner, in 120 characters or fewer. An AI judge compares lines two at a time and picks the funnier, fresher, tighter one.

Entrants write one line, at most 120 characters: the error message a kitchen toaster shows its owner when something goes wrong. It can be deadpan, apologetic, quietly dramatic or plain absurd, as long as it reads like a real message on a real appliance and makes someone laugh. Each entry is that single text, sent in by the entrant. A judge model compares two entries at a time on funniness, freshness and tightness, in private rounds, and the best line takes the reward.

Your program writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`, and the judge below compares the entries two at a time by its brief, published here in full.
